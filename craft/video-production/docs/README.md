# Video production — craft reference

An educational, **tool-agnostic** reference on how video production works: the vocabulary, the craft, and the conventions, for anyone (human or agent) who writes, reviews, or builds videos in this repo. Nothing here is specific to our engine, our tools, or any vendor — it is the general craft. Where our pipeline names a concept differently, that mapping lives with the engine specs, not here.

## Why this exists

The engine produces videos; the people and agents steering it need a shared, correct understanding of video-production craft so reviews, storyboards, and briefs use words the same way and aim at the same bar. This is that common ground.

## Contents

- **[glossary.md](glossary.md)** — the vocabulary: timeline, track, clip, asset, scene, shot, cut, transition, plate, compositing, layer, PiP, voiceover, soundtrack, SFX, VFX, render, and the rest. Start here.
- **[anatomy-of-a-launch-video.md](anatomy-of-a-launch-video.md)** — how a software launch video is structured: the arc, length by placement, hooks, proof, the CTA.
- **[showing-a-web-app.md](showing-a-web-app.md)** — how to portray a web or mobile app on screen: device framing (bare / browser / device shell), real vs stylized fidelity, directing the eye, interaction as motion, and the sound of the interface.
