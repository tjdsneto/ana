# Review rubric — research / coverage (conformance axis)

Loaded by the generic `review-conformance` reviewer when judging a `research` deliverable. The generic reviewer owns the ship-test framing and the VERDICT mechanics; this file is ONLY the research-specific coverage rubric.

**Minimal starter rubric.** Research has no dedicated source skill yet; this is a first-principles starter, not a mature rubric. Expand it as the research deliverable's own spec/skill matures.

Does the research dossier give downstream steps the raw material the brief needs?

- **Serves the brief's needs.** The dossier supplies the raw material later steps (moodboard, script, storyboard) actually need — the brand's real product, palette, era, audience, and claims — rather than generic filler.
- **Grounded, not invented.** Every fact is sourced from the real brand/product, not fabricated. No invented product details, numbers, palettes, or claims.
- **Covers what the brief calls for.** Each thing the brief asks research to establish is present; nothing the brief requires is missing.

On revise, name what the brief needed that the dossier missed, or which fact is ungrounded/invented.

Build on: `../deliverable-research.md` if it exists (it exists for: script, moodboard) and the matching §B profile. Reference rather than restate.
