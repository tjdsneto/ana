# Review rubric — assemble / consistency

Loaded by the generic `review-consistency` reviewer when judging a `assemble` deliverable. The generic reviewer owns the ship-test framing and the VERDICT mechanics; this file is ONLY the assemble-specific consistency rubric.

> **Minimal starter rubric** — no source skill exists for assemble consistency yet; this is written from first principles and should be hardened once assemble consistency is exercised on real films.

You review the **assembled film as a whole** on the **consistency** axis — do the stitched scenes read as one film, or as a set of pieces that drift apart? You judge the film across its scenes, not any single scene's own craft.

Check:
- **Shared look held across every scene** — each scene carries the moodboard's look: its palette, the one sourced font, and the shared ground/surface treatment. No scene is off-palette, on a different face, or on a treatment the others don't use.
- **Beats flow in order, no jarring style break** — scene-to-scene the film holds its tempo and visual register; there's no abrupt shift in look, motion energy, or pacing at a seam that pulls the viewer out.
- **No scene contradicts the established signature/look** — nothing a later scene does undoes the look the opening scenes establish (a recurring lockup/caption style/transition that suddenly changes, an accent that flips, a mood that reverses).

On revise, name which scene (or seam) departs from the established look + the concrete fix, so just that scene is reopened rather than the whole assembly redone.

Build on: the **moodboard** (the shared look), `../a1-frame.md`, and the matching §B profile. Reference rather than restate.
