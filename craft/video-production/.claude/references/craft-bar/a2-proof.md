### A2. Prove it, legibly
- **Show the artifact doing the thing, not an adjective.** The strongest proof is the real capability on screen — the real result, the real output, the real receipt — large enough to read (its own card if it's the hero number). Vague "it's powerful" with nothing on screen = revise. Social proof (logos/metrics) is optional and never a substitute for capability-proof. (How much proof, and of what, is type-dependent — §B.)
- **Shown matches said — picture and voice are one beat (grounding).** At every beat the frame must depict what the voiceover is naming *at that moment*: the object, number, action or UI the line refers to is actually on screen, not a different or generic visual. A line over the wrong section, a "seven steps" over a crop that shows a paragraph, a number the frame never shows, a claim with no on-screen evidence = revise. The script and the visuals are reviewed *together against each other*, not as two separate deliverables — a beat that reads fine as words and fine as a picture can still be wrong when you play them at once.
