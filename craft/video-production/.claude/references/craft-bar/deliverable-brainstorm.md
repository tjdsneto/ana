# Deliverable rubric — brainstorm (the concept seeds)

The brainstorm's own grading criteria — distinct creative concepts for the video, each a genuinely different angle. Builds on `a3-slop.md` (grounding + anti-slop) and the technique palette in `techniques.json` (what the engine can actually build).

- **Divergence.** The concepts are genuinely distinct angles (structure / hook / metaphor), not rephrasings of one idea. Same-idea-reworded is a miss.
- **Goal fit.** Each concept serves the brief's goal and can land the brief's required messages. A clever angle that can't carry the message is not a candidate.
- **Buildability.** Each concept leans on a capability this engine can make — cross-check `techniques.json` feasibility (real-UI-capture, kinetic SVG cards, a generative clip, VO/chips), not something unbuildable. A concept whose technique is `roadmap:*` or `needs-user-footage` is flagged, not seeded as now-buildable.
- **Spec quality.** Each concept's brief is self-contained and concrete — the angle, the hook, a rough beat arc, and which capability it leans on.

Grounding is a guardrail (`a3-slop.md`): a concept must not invent facts or fabricate proof (a no-skill brainstorm once invented fake results) — prevent at produce, don't reject-and-redo.
