# Deliverable rubric — plan-scenes (beats → realized scenes)

The scene-plan's own grading criteria — one scene per storyboard beat, each with a realization method. Builds on `a1-frame.md` and `a2-proof.md`, plus the production's `b-*.md` profile.

- **Mapping.** Exactly one entry per storyboard beat, in playback order. No invented messages, no merged or split beats. Name a beat that was dropped or doubled.
- **Method fit.** Each scene's method is from the palette (`authored-composite` · `whole-clip-generative` · `authored-continuous` · `edit-footage` · `motion-graphics`) and sensibly chosen for that beat — not one method blindly applied to all.
- **Spec quality.** Each scene brief is self-contained and concrete: the subject, the spoken line(s), the visual direction carried from the storyboard, and a duration.
- **Anchors carried to the right scene.** The §A1 signature move lands on the one scene that holds it; the hero number is framed big on its scene (`a1-frame.md`); the §B close (a launch's CTA) is in the final scene. An anchor that exists in the storyboard but reaches no scene is a miss.
